Questions patients ask

How do I book an appointment?

Use the form above, call 087545 03505 or message us on WhatsApp. We will contact you to confirm a time.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9:30 am–1:30 pm and 4:30–9 pm. Sunday, 10 am–1:30 pm and 5–9 pm. Hours may change on holidays, so call ahead.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any previous dental records or X-rays, and a list of medicines you take.

Do you treat the whole family?

We describe ourselves as a family dental clinic. Call to confirm for specific age groups.

Do you offer crowns and bridges?

Yes. We list metal ceramic crowns with a 10-year warranty and zirconia bridges. Ask the doctor which is right for you.
